# Prompt de generación

Modo usado: herramienta integrada `image_gen`.

## Fondo del gráfico destacado

Use case: ads-marketing  
Asset type: Google Play feature graphic background for a Spanish word puzzle game  
Primary request: Create a polished playful 3D abstract background inspired by arranging word tiles into connected groups.  
Scene/backdrop: warm cream studio backdrop with soft coral gradient accents and a subtle curved path.  
Subject: floating rounded square word tiles shown only as blank shapes, arranged in small groups, with a few subtle colorful connection lines and confetti-like geometric pieces.  
Style/medium: premium friendly 3D clay render, clean mobile-game branding, tactile soft materials.  
Composition/framing: very wide horizontal composition; keep the central-left area calm and readable for later logo placement; decorative tile clusters mostly around the edges; strong visual balance at 1024x500.  
Lighting/mood: soft warm studio lighting, clever, welcoming, energetic.  
Color palette: cream #FBF7F0, coral #EB5B43, mustard #F4B63F, sage #59B7A4, dark ink #242322 used sparingly.  
Constraints: no characters, no mascot, no letters, no words, no logo, no watermark, no phone mockup; blank tiles only; avoid clutter; preserve generous negative space for later typography.

La mascota, el logotipo, la tipografía, las capturas y los tamaños finales se compusieron de manera determinística a partir de los recursos del proyecto.
